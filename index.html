MassiveShift College Academy
Registered TVET College | Non-Profit Community Foundation

MassiveShift is a job-focused College Academy and a supportive College Community, reshaping private education for Africa's young population. We deliver skills training that leads directly to employment or entrepreneurship — with speed, accountability, and a lifelong network.

Our model is simple: teach only what leads to a job or a business. 70% practical, 30% theory. Graduate with a certificate, portfolio, and alumni network. We are registered with TEVETA (Zambia) and partner with local employers for internships and placements.
