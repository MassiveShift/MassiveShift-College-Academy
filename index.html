About MassiveShift

We are a registered private TVET college and a non-profit community foundation, built to close Africa's skills-to-jobs gap.

MassiveShift is not just a college — it's a movement. We offer short, intensive certificate programs (4–12 months) in high-demand fields: solar installation, digital marketing, mobile repair, and entrepreneurship.

Our model is simple: teach only what leads to a job or a business. 70% practical, 30% theory. Graduate with a certificate, portfolio, and alumni network.

We are registered with TEVETA (Zambia) and partner with local employers for internships and placements. The MassiveShift Community Foundation provides free mentorship, job boards, and peer support to graduates and the public.

Our Mission

Equip 100,000 African youth with job-ready skills by 2028, while fostering an ecosystem of lifelong learning and entrepreneurship.
